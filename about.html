Our story

A little sweetness
for every day.

New Mummy Bakery is built around a simple belief: the best celebrations do not need to be complicated. Sometimes it is a warm puff on the way home, a sweet box carried into a family gathering, or a cake waiting quietly at the centre of the table.

Our counter brings together familiar favourites and small moments of delight — sweets with a soft, generous finish, cakes made for milestones, and savouries that are best enjoyed fresh from the oven.

Across Ramanathapuram, we keep the experience warm, personal and easy: choose what feels right, ask us anything, and we will help you take a little more joy home.
