# New Mummy Bakery — Design Direction

## Theme Name
**The Warm Counter** — a refined South Indian bakery identity that feels like stepping up to a beautifully lit shop counter at golden hour.

## Direction
- **Design movement:** editorial food-brand minimalism with tactile, hand-finished details.
- **Core principles:** generous whitespace, confident type scale, warm contrast, simple paths to order.
- **Color philosophy:** cream and soft sand for the canvas; cocoa for readable type; terracotta for appetite and action; pistachio for freshness and category accents.
- **Layout paradigm:** asymmetric editorial hero, dense-but-breathable product grid, wide location band, sticky mobile ordering action.
- **Signature elements:** oversized serif headlines, thin rule dividers, pill category tabs, product cards with abstract ingredient illustrations.
- **Interaction philosophy:** immediate, low-friction, friendly; add-to-order actions surface feedback without blocking browsing.
- **Animation:** subtle rise/fade on cards, soft hover lifts, reduced-motion safe transitions.
- **Typography system:** Cormorant Garamond for display; DM Sans for UI/body; Tamil copy in a system sans fallback.
- **Brand essence:** daily freshness, celebratory generosity, Ramanathapuram familiarity.
- **Brand voice:** warm, specific, quietly premium; never overclaims.
- **Wordmark/logo:** MB monogram with a stylized oven flame / folded pastry shape.
- **Signature brand color:** deep cocoa #2D211B.
